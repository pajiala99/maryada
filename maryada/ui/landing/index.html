The problem

Most recovery calls are never heard by anyone but the two people on them.

Lenders make recovery calls at a volume no team can listen to, through outsourced agents, in many languages, on noisy phone lines. Quality teams sample a few. When a borrower complains, someone has to find the call, listen to all of it, and prove what was said.

VolumeEvery agent makes many calls a day. Listening to each one end to end is not possible.

Languages and linesCalls mix Hindi and English, or are in Punjabi, Tamil, Bengali and more, on 8 kHz lines with a busy floor behind the agent.

The day someone asksA complaint, an internal audit or a regulator's query needs the exact words, the time, and the rule they broke.

Without and with Maryada

The same complaint, two ways.

Without

  1. Search the dialer's recordings by hand for the borrower's calls.
  2. Listen to each one from start to end.
  3. Write up what was said and which rule it broke.
  4. Assemble the recording, the notes and the loan record into evidence.

With Maryada

  1. The complaint is matched to the borrower's calls automatically.
  2. The breach is already marked: the moment, the clause and the exact words.
  3. The reviewer confirms it, and the evidence pack builds from that review.
  4. An action on the agent is recorded against the finding.

What it checks

Six things an agent may not do on a recovery call.

Call outside 08:00 to 19:00Unless the borrower asked for another time.Contact hours (para 100W(4))
No recording noticeThe borrower must be told the call is recorded.Call recording (para 100N)
Threats or abuseNo intimidation, insults, or threats to person, family or property.Harsh practices (para 100X(1),(5),(7))
The wrong amountAny figure the agent states must match the loan record.Harsh practices (para 100X(8))
Relatives, employer, public shamingDues are discussed with the borrower only.Harsh practices (para 100X(2),(6)); para 100W(1)
Pressing at a sensitive timeBack off during a death, a calamity or a wedding.Inappropriate occasions (para 100W(6))

Clause numbers follow published summaries of the RBI directions of 6 August 2026 and should be checked against the RBI's own text.

For developers

Plugs into the dialer you already run.

Send a stereo recording and the loan record; get back the findings with the RBI clause, the exact words and the evidence seal. Every alert is also posted to your server as a signed webhook. Names and account numbers are masked before any text reaches the LLM.

curl -X POST https://your-maryada/v1/calls \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer mk_test_..." \
  -F audio=@call.wav \
  -F 'metadata={"borrower_name": "...", "lender": "...",
     "language": "hi-en", "call_time": "...",
     "loan": {"emi": 2450, "overdue_emis": 1,
              "late_fee": 500, "total_due": 2950}}'

{"id": "call_...", "status": "queued", "mode": "test"}

Honest limits

What this is, and what it is not yet.

  • Every call is synthetic. Timbre voices are cleaner than real people, so real calls will score lower.
  • Noise is mixed in digitally; there is no real handset, network jitter or packet loss.
  • Recordings are assumed to be stereo, one side per channel.
  • The LLM judge is a stand-in model; Gnani's Evon v3.3 was not on the API yet and was not measured.
  • The RBI text was read through published summaries. This is an automated check, not legal advice.
  • Lines beyond Hinglish and Punjabi were written by us and not checked by native speakers.

Maryada (मर्यादा) means dignity: staying within limits.
